Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions about Multi-Language Audio Transcription and MP3 Cutting.

1. What audio formats are supported?

You can upload MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC (WhatsApp voice notes), FLAC, OGG, and WEBM files. You can also record directly using your microphone.

2. Can I cut a specific part of a long song or interview and convert only that part to text?

Yes! Open the '✂️ Cutter' tab, select your Start and End time on the waveform, click 'Cut Audio Now', and then press 'Convert to Text'.
